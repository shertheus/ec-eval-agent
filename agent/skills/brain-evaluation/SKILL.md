description: Configure, execute and inspect reproducible brain connectivity comparisons.
---

Use the registered domain tools. Start by inspecting capabilities and existing experiments.
Prepare or import observations, then validate the complete protocol before submission.
Record method-specific assumptions. gCastle algorithms may be applied to brain time series;
their static treatment of observations must remain explicit. Do not invent time order for IID samples.
Preserve failures. Treat every number in the narrative as requiring a run/metric evidence reference.
Do not tune on final ground truth. Never describe a short installation demo as a converged benchmark.
When CUDA is requested but unavailable, explain the error; do not silently change the device.
User conclusions and course reflections must be based on their own interpretation of the evidence.
